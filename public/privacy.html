Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Back to cerebel.tech

In short: this website counts visits without cookies and without storing your IP address. If you join the glasses presale list, we keep your email only to tell you about the presale. We never sell your information and we use no advertising trackers.

This policy covers cerebel.tech, the website of Cerebel, Inc. ("Cerebel", "we", "us"). The swing analysis app at cerebel.tech/playground has its own Privacy Policy, which covers accounts, uploaded videos and body data.

1. What we collect

  • Visits. When you open a page, when you click a link or button, and every 30 seconds while the page is open in front of you, your browser sends us: a random ID kept in your browser's local storage (not a cookie, and not tied to your name), the page path without any query string, the domain of the website that sent you, any campaign tags in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign), the text of the button or link you clicked, your country, your time zone, your device type (computer, phone or tablet) and your browser family. We do not store your IP address or your full browser identification string with this data. Visits from automated crawlers are not recorded.
  • Glasses presale list. If you join it: your email address, the name and intended use you choose to give, the time, your country, the page you joined from, the domain of the website that sent you, and the same random browser ID, so we can tell which pages lead people to join.
  • Emails you send us, with whatever you choose to write.

2. How we use it

  • To count visitors and understand what brings people to the site and which pages work.
  • To write to people on the presale list about the presale, such as when it opens, the price and the timing. Joining is free and does not commit you to buy.
  • To answer your messages.

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Clearing your browser's site data, or blocking local storage, gives you a new random ID. The site works the same either way.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to be removed from the presale list, by writing to hello@cerebel.tech. Every presale email will also tell you how to unsubscribe. We answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking.
  • California residents: in the last 12 months we collected identifiers (email address and a random browser ID), internet activity (pages viewed and clicks) and approximate location (country) from you and your browser, for the purposes in section 2, and disclosed them only to the providers in section 3. We do not sell or share personal information, including of people under 16. You have the right to know, delete and correct your information, and an authorized agent may ask on your behalf.

6. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has joined the presale list, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects the presale list in a significant way, we will also tell the people on it.
