# Motionverse AI Website Optimization Plan

## 1. Objective

Turn the current visually strong prototype into a concise, trustworthy, conversion-ready public website.

The optimized site should help a new visitor understand four things quickly:

1. What Motionverse does.
2. Why it is different.
3. What evidence supports the claim.
4. What the visitor should do next.

The existing black, warm-white, signal-orange, and data-blue visual system should remain. The optimization should focus on narrative structure, credibility, mobile density, conversion, and accessibility rather than introducing another visual redesign.

## 2. Desired outcome

- Move concrete product evidence earlier in the journey.
- Reduce the main page from 12 sections to approximately 8 focused sections.
- Remove every unfinished or placeholder state.
- Make Benchmark or Proof directly accessible from navigation.
- Shorten the mobile path from the benchmark introduction to the first interactive chart.
- Clarify the company's differentiated position above the fold.
- Improve demo-request completion and confidence.
- Establish WCAG 2.2 AA as the accessibility baseline.

## 3. Recommended information architecture

### 01 — Hero

**Purpose:** Explain the company and create a clear next action.

- Keep the current cinematic hero and primary headline.
- Bring the core statement into the first viewport:
  - “We make human physical intelligence legible to machines.”
- Keep “Book a Demo” as the primary action.
- Change the secondary action to “See the Proof” and link it to the synchronized evidence section.
- Integrate the most important “data gap” point into the hero transition instead of keeping a full standalone section.

### 02 — Reconstruction Evidence

**Purpose:** Show that the product produces inspectable output.

- Move the synchronized egocentric and SMPL demonstration directly after the hero.
- Keep the two views on one timeline.
- Retain the development-demo caveat.
- Add one concise explanation of what a reviewer should notice:
  - body recovery;
  - temporal continuity;
  - relationship between camera context and reconstructed motion.

### 03 — AMASS Benchmark

**Purpose:** Provide transparent quantitative credibility.

- Keep the interactive ranking explorer.
- Keep direct values, sequence switching, method selection, and the source table.
- Add “Benchmark” as a direct desktop and mobile navigation destination.
- On mobile, condense Dataset, Protocol, Reference Field, and Status into one summary rail.
- Put detailed methodology inside an expandable panel so the first chart appears earlier.
- Keep Motionverse results withheld until the protocol and values are reproducible.

### 04 — How Motionverse Works

**Purpose:** Explain the system after visitors have seen proof.

- Keep the four-stage flow: Capture, Understand, Structure, Deploy.
- Remove duplicated explanation already covered by the hero or evidence section.
- Keep the active-step interaction and image relationship.
- Reduce the overall height of the section by tightening inactive states and copy.

### 05 — Technology Platform

**Purpose:** Explain the integrated hardware and software platform.

- Combine the current Technology Stack and Wearable Platform stories.
- Preserve conceptual/development labels.
- Show the platform as three coordinated layers:
  1. Wearable capture.
  2. Perception and reconstruction.
  3. Structured data infrastructure.
- Avoid detailed performance, file-format, readiness, or hardware claims until verified.

### 06 — Applications

**Purpose:** Connect the platform to valuable use cases.

- Merge the standalone Robotics section into Application Scenarios.
- Keep Robotics as the default selected application.
- Retain Industrial Operations, Sports Intelligence, and Research + Digital Humans as selectable states.
- For each state, show:
  - the captured activity;
  - the useful structured information;
  - the intended downstream workflow.
- Avoid customer, partnership, or deployment claims unless confirmed.

### 07 — Company and Partnership Model

**Purpose:** Explain why and how an organization would work with Motionverse.

- Combine the current Partner Solutions and Company Vision sections.
- Separate possible engagement types clearly:
  - research collaboration;
  - data collection study;
  - product or hardware partnership;
  - strategic discussion.
- Keep language exploratory unless commercial readiness is verified.

### 08 — Book a Demo

**Purpose:** Convert qualified visitors.

- Remove the visible contact instruction and all placeholders.
- Confirm the final contact email before launch.
- Use only real LinkedIn, X, and Privacy destinations; remove links that are not ready.
- Reduce the initial form burden:
  - Work email;
  - Organization;
  - Area of interest;
  - Message as optional.
- Explain what happens after submission without making an unverified response-time promise.
- Add a real Privacy link and a short data-use reassurance.

## 4. Navigation plan

### Desktop

Recommended order:

1. Technology
2. Evidence
3. Benchmark
4. Applications
5. Company
6. Book a Demo

Keep the compact top bar and vertical section-progress indicator.

### Mobile

Use the same destinations and terminology as desktop:

1. Technology
2. Evidence
3. Benchmark
4. Applications
5. Company
6. Book a Demo

Keep the designed full-screen menu, large targets, visible close control, and bottom product statement.

## 5. Visual and content refinements

### Preserve

- Black and warm-white editorial contrast.
- Signal orange for current selection and action.
- Data blue for the published leader or better direction.
- Cinematic real-world activity.
- Technical annotations, timelines, rules, and restrained square geometry.
- One major idea per section.

### Refine

- Use fewer oversized section introductions after the first three sections.
- Alternate proof-led, compact, and image-led layouts to reduce repetition.
- Increase dark-theme body-copy contrast one step.
- Keep essential body text at a comfortable mobile size.
- Reserve very small monospace text for nonessential annotations.
- Keep technical language concise and define unfamiliar metrics at the point of use.

## 6. Trust and launch-readiness checklist

### Must be completed before public launch

- Remove the direct-contact instruction placeholder.
- Replace or remove LinkedIn, X, and Privacy placeholders.
- Confirm the final company email.
- Verify every navigation and footer destination.
- Confirm which product images are conceptual, development, or current.
- Confirm benchmark source wording and canonical citation.
- Verify form success, error, and duplicate-submission states.
- Confirm privacy and data-retention language.

### Content that must remain withheld until verified

- Motionverse benchmark scores.
- Detailed performance or latency claims.
- Detailed file-format promises.
- Customers, partnerships, or deployments.
- Product availability or readiness claims.

## 7. Accessibility and responsive plan

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the public website.

- Check text and non-text contrast across black, warm-white, orange, and blue states.
- Keep visible keyboard focus on every interactive control.
- Ensure all navigation, tabs, benchmark rows, disclosure panels, and form fields work by keyboard.
- Mark required fields explicitly in text and semantics.
- Associate every error with its field and announce submission status.
- Verify heading order and landmark structure.
- Provide useful video alternatives and captions where necessary.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences for parallax, scroll transitions, and chart animation.
- Test reflow and zoom at 200%.
- Validate at minimum:
  - 390 × 844 mobile;
  - 768px tablet;
  - 1440 × 1000 desktop.

## 8. Delivery phases

### Phase 0 — Credibility cleanup

**Estimated effort:** half a day

- Remove public placeholders.
- Confirm contact and footer destinations.
- Remove any link that is not ready.
- Verify direct-contact and privacy copy.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- No visitor-facing placeholder text remains.
- Every visible link has a real destination.
- Contact identity is confirmed.

### Phase 1 — Narrative and navigation

**Estimated effort:** 1–2 days

- Reorder the page around evidence-first storytelling.
- Reduce 12 main sections to approximately 8.
- Update desktop and mobile navigation.
- Bring the core brand statement into the hero.
- Change the secondary hero action to “See the Proof.”

**Acceptance criteria:**

- A first-time visitor can reach qualitative proof from the hero in one action.
- Benchmark is directly accessible from navigation.
- No section repeats the primary job of another section.

### Phase 2 — Section consolidation

**Estimated effort:** 2 days

- Merge Technology Stack with Wearable Platform.
- Merge Robotics with Applications.
- Merge Partner Solutions with Company Vision.
- Tighten copy and vertical spacing.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Each section expresses one primary idea.
- The mobile page is materially shorter.
- The existing design language remains recognizable.

### Phase 3 — Benchmark and conversion optimization

**Estimated effort:** 1–2 days

- Compress mobile benchmark protocol.
- Bring the chart into view earlier.
- Simplify demo-request fields.
- Add submission reassurance and privacy context.
- Verify all form states without sending unintended leads.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- The first interactive benchmark view appears substantially earlier on mobile.
- Required and optional fields are clear.
- Error and success states are understandable and accessible.

### Phase 4 — Accessibility and final QA

**Estimated effort:** 1 day

- Contrast, keyboard, zoom, reduced-motion, and responsive testing.
- Browser and device checks.
- Broken-link and console review.
- Final content verification.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- No P0/P1 UX or accessibility findings remain.
- No console warnings or errors appear in the tested public journey.
- Production build and Sites tests pass.

### Total suggested implementation window

Approximately 5–7 working days, depending on how quickly final contact, privacy, citation, and product-readiness details are confirmed.

## 9. Measurement plan

Track improvement without inventing success claims:

- Hero “See the Proof” click-through rate.
- Evidence video engagement.
- Benchmark metric, sequence, and method interactions.
- Book-a-Demo clicks originating before and after proof sections.
- Form starts and successful submissions.
- Mobile scroll depth to Evidence, Benchmark, and Demo.
- Submission quality by area of interest.

Analytics should avoid unnecessary personal data and follow the confirmed privacy policy.

## 10. Inputs required from the Motionverse team

1. Confirmed public contact email.
2. Final LinkedIn and X destinations, or a decision to remove them.
3. Privacy policy destination and approved data-use wording.
4. Canonical benchmark citation.
5. Confirmation of which visual assets are conceptual versus current development configurations.
6. Approval of the core hero statement and page order.
7. Decision on which demo-request fields are truly required.

## 11. Definition of done

The optimization is complete when:

- the website contains no placeholders;
- the company differentiator is visible above the fold;
- qualitative and quantitative proof appear before extended platform explanation;
- the page contains approximately eight focused sections;
- Benchmark is directly accessible from navigation;
- the mobile benchmark reaches the chart faster;
- the demo path is credible, concise, and accessible;
- all claims and product-status language are verified;
- accessibility and responsive checks pass;
- the production build and Sites test suite pass.
